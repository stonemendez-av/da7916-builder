1 NavLog

Flight Documents Required

  1. Enter the flight plan under the FPL tab.
  2. Tap the share button under the flight plan.
  3. Select Flights in the 'Send to' pop-up window.
  4. Go to the Flights tab in ForeFlight and share the PDF.
  5. Save the file.

ForeFlight Mobile - NavLog Recommended

  1. Enter the flight plan under the FPL tab.
  2. Tap the share button under the flight plan.
  3. Select Print, then tap the share button in the top right of the print pop-up window.
  4. Save the file.

    3 Waypoints

    Pick each point's type: SP, ACP, CP and RP take a number, LZs take a bird name and PZs a tree name. A number or name can only be used once per type. Airports fill in as their identifier. MGRS grids come from the Mobile NavLog when it's loaded; otherwise type them in (the regular NavLog's coordinates are rounded to the nearest minute, so they're only shown as a reference). Every point needs a grid before you can build. LAT LONG is converted from the grid.
